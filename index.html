    AI-enabled risk management for the energy industry

    Hua Lu Risk Management & Technology Consulting Co. develops AI-enabled risk management technology that helps organizations identify emerging risks, understand potential impacts and make faster, better-informed decisions.

    The platform combines artificial intelligence, data analytics and enterprise risk management methodology to help energy companies identify, assess, monitor and manage complex risk. Critical risk information that usually sits across spreadsheets, reports, legacy systems and business functions is brought into a single, continuously updated view.
